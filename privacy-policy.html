Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 1, 2026  |  Handy Helper Company LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Handy Helper Company LLC ("Handy Helper," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website at handyhelper.company, our Android mobile application, and our AI-powered home repair assistant (collectively, the "Service"). By using the Service you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you use the Service:

  • Questions and messages you type into the AI assistant
  • Photos and images you voluntarily upload for analysis
  • Contact information such as your name and email address if you contact us directly
  • Information you submit through our contact form or community board

We also collect information automatically when you use the Service:

  • Usage data including pages visited, features used, and time spent on the Service
  • Device information including device type, operating system, and browser type
  • Log data including IP address, access times, and referring URLs
  • Analytics data collected through Google Analytics

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, operate, and improve the Service
  • Process and respond to your home repair questions and requests
  • Analyze photos you upload to provide tailored guidance
  • Understand how users interact with the Service to improve functionality
  • Respond to your comments, questions, and requests
  • Send you technical notices and support messages
  • Monitor and analyze usage patterns and trends
  • Detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent or harmful activity
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Sharing of Information

We share your information with the following third parties to operate the Service:

  • Anthropic, Inc. — Our AI assistant is powered by Anthropic's Claude API. Questions you ask and photos you upload are processed by Anthropic's servers to generate responses. Anthropic's privacy policy is available at anthropic.com/privacy.
  • Google Analytics — We use Google Analytics to collect and analyze usage data. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Streamlit — Our AI chatbot is hosted on Streamlit Community Cloud. Streamlit's privacy policy is available at streamlit.io/privacy-policy.
  • Vercel — Our website is hosted on Vercel. Vercel's privacy policy is available at vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process.

4. Photos and Images

When you upload a photo to Handy Helper for analysis the image is transmitted to Anthropic's Claude API for processing and then discarded. We do not permanently store the photos you upload. Photos are used solely to provide you with relevant home repair guidance and are not used for any other purpose.

Do not upload photos that contain personally identifiable information of other individuals, sensitive financial documents, or any content that is illegal or violates the rights of others.

5. Data Retention

We retain conversation data for the duration of your active session. Sessions are not permanently stored on our servers. When you close or refresh the application your conversation history is cleared.

Analytics data collected through Google Analytics is retained according to Google's standard data retention policies.

6. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13 please contact us immediately at yourhelper@handyhelper.company and we will take steps to delete such information.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmitted between your device and our Service is encrypted using industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information.

8. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location you may have certain rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access — You may request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction — You may request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Deletion — You may request that we delete your personal information
  • Opt-out of analytics — You may opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout

To exercise any of these rights please contact us at yourhelper@handyhelper.company.

9. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). You have the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your California privacy rights contact us at yourhelper@handyhelper.company.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Your continued use of the Service after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and analyze usage patterns. You can control cookie settings through your browser preferences. Disabling cookies may affect some features of the Service.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — necessary for the Service to function properly
  • Analytics cookies — used by Google Analytics to understand how users interact with the Service
